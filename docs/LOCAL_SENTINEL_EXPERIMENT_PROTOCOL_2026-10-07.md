# Local Sentinel-2 extension: analysis specification

Written after inspecting catalog metadata and a cloud-obscured test window, before computing the time-series spectral outcomes. This is an internal analysis specification, not an externally registered study.

- Fixed geographic domain: full recovered UAV footprint, W117.404195622047, S23.9134768474132, E117.432930102047, N23.9374309174132. Report the historical ROI separately. Site10 coordinates are rounded to 0.01 degrees; its possible rounding cell is context, not a recovered plot footprint.
- Years 2019–2025, September only: a common season with Sentinel-2 L2A coverage and a match to the reported Fujian September 2025 campaign. This does not provide contemporaneous ground labels.
- Candidate inclusion: all intersecting Earth Search sentinel-2-c1-l2a items. Select using local SCL only, without looking at NDVI. SCL 4,5,6 define the provisional clear set. Rank by full-footprint clear fraction, retain at most one granule/date and at most three dates/year, each >=80% clear. Break ties by item ID. Report all candidates and exclusions.
- Retain unscaled native DN windows for B02/B03/B04/B08/B11 and categorical SCL. Decode per-asset scale and offset from STAC; check provider guidance to avoid processing-baseline artifacts. Nonzero DN and positive red/NIR sum are required for NDVI. Do not silently clip negative physical reflectances.
- Common analysis grid: exact native 10 m red-band grid. Resample 20 m SCL and B11 by nearest neighbour; do not imply additional spatial information. Verify all 10 m bands share alignment. Geometric matching is metadata based, not ground-control validation.
- Compute per-date NDVI and MNDWI. Primary mask excludes all SCL classes other than 4,5,6 and excludes the 20 m neighbourhood of rejected SCL cells. Sensitivity: no cloud-edge buffer. Water remains part of the clear domain; excluding it would hide a potential change in vegetation/inundation. Record water-index and SCL-water fractions separately.
- For each year, use pixelwise medians of available selected dates. Compare the same pixels valid in every available year, with a >=2 observations/year sensitivity. Report how much area remains and all missing years. Pixel counts are not independent biological replicates; no pixel-level causal P values.
- Spatial summaries: full UAV footprint, historical ROI, and fixed baseline CCAV-2019 classes for S. alterniflora and mangroves. CCAV classes are stratification labels, not verified species truth. Do not update the strata using later outcomes. No training on CCAV labels and no claim of independent species validation.
- Outputs: per-scene metadata/quality table, raw spatial subsets and digests, annual composites and valid-count rasters, fixed-support summaries, sensitivity table and map/time-series figure. September maxima/minima among acquisition dates diagnose observation variability, not confidence intervals.
- Interpretation: later spectral greenness and water exposure of fixed locations. No inference of genet identity, mortality, evolutionary adaptation or eradication causality. Local SCL errors, cloud adjacency, tides, atmospheric correction and registration remain alternatives.

Product amendment before spectral outcome analysis: legacy sentinel-2-l2a has reported inconsistent offset metadata (Earth Search issues 9, 66, 71). Legacy SCL download is an acquisition diagnostic only. Use Collection 1 L2A for the experiment, re-screen its own SCL, and preserve the legacy issue evidence. Do not combine the two products into a trend.

Radiometry rule fixed before index calculation: exclude negative red or NIR physical reflectances from NDVI, and negative green/SWIR from MNDWI; record exclusions instead of truncating values. Collection 1 source product XML must pass provider SHA-256 and confirm the STAC scale/offset for all five bands. Acquisition screening found only one eligible 2020 date; retain it in the primary analysis, and additionally report >=2 observations/year common support across years that actually have >=2 selected dates. Do not present this as a full seven-year sensitivity.

Additional acquisition sensitivity specified before spectral outcome calculation: compare all 2019×2025 selected-date pairs on pixels clear/valid in every endpoint acquisition and in the primary common support. The range across pairs diagnoses dependence on imaging date; pairs are not independent replicates.
