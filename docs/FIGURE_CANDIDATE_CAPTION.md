# Candidate caption for `fig_patch_dynamics_audit.pdf`

**Fig. X | Auditing the available annual patch-record archive.** **A**, Number of records returned by the local patch table (bars) and median segmented-area proxy (line). Record counts are detection outputs and are not estimates of population size. **B**, Distribution of log10(new-record area / putative parent area) for years with more than one observed parent size. The 2020–2021 parent-size plateau is shown as an audit flag and is excluded from this normalized comparison. **C**, Median and interquartile range of the recorded parent-distance proxy. The 2021 constant distance value is shown as an audit flag rather than as a biological estimate. **D**, July climate context from a NASA POWER point series at approximately 117.60° E, 23.95° N, plotted as standardized annual values alongside the patch-ratio summary. This panel is exploratory and does not establish an environmental cause. All source values, flags and retrieval metadata are provided in `tables/` and `external_data/`.
